# Cyber Incident Report: U.S. Congressional Budget Office Intrusion (Nov 9, 2025)

## Overview
In November 2025, the U.S. Congressional Budget Office (CBO) confirmed that its network had been compromised in a cyber intrusion. While the agency stated that the incident was contained and that operations continued without disruption, it has not publicly disclosed the full scope of systems or data affected.

---

## Victim Organization
- **Name:** U.S. Congressional Budget Office (CBO)  
- **Sector:** Federal Government  
- **Role:** Provides nonpartisan budgetary and economic analysis to the U.S. Congress

---

## Facts (What Happened?)
The CBO publicly acknowledged that it experienced a cyber intrusion into its network. Following detection, the agency implemented enhanced monitoring and additional security controls to contain the incident. As of the initial disclosure, the CBO has not released details regarding which systems were accessed or whether any data was exfiltrated.

The investigation remains ongoing.

---

## Timeline
- **November 6, 2025:** CBO publicly discloses the cyber intrusion  
- **November 6, 2025:** News outlets report on the incident shortly after the announcement  
- **Ongoing:** Investigation and forensic analysis continue

---

## Motive
The motive remains **unknown**. Early media reporting has suggested the possibility of foreign-state involvement, but this has not been confirmed by the CBO or investigators.

---

## Damages & Impact
- **Operational impact:** No reported disruption; CBO operations continue uninterrupted  
- **Data impact:** Scope of data access remains undisclosed  
- **Risk exposure:** Potential compromise of sensitive economic models, budget projections, and internal policy analyses

---

## Attribution
- **Threat actor:** Unknown  
- **Suspected involvement:** Possible foreign actor (unconfirmed)  
- **Attribution status:** No official attribution at the time of reporting

---

## Analysis & Opinion
This incident is particularly concerning given the sensitivity of the CBO’s work, which directly informs national fiscal and economic policy decisions. Even in the absence of operational disruption, unauthorized access to internal models or analyses could carry long-term strategic and political implications. While limited disclosure may be necessary during an active investigation, maintaining public trust will require timely and transparent updates as more information becomes available. The breach underscores the continued attractiveness of federal agencies as high-value targets and the importance of sustained investment in proactive cybersecurity defenses.
