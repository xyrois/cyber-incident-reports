# Cyber Incident Reports

This repository contains independent analyses of real-world cybersecurity incidents and data breaches.

The reports are structured using a standardized investigative framework that examines:
- What happened
- When it happened
- Why it happened
- Who may be responsible
- The resulting impact
- Lessons learned and risk implications

These analyses were originally developed during academic coursework and have been revised, expanded, and reformatted for public and professional use.

---

## Methodology

Each incident report follows a consistent structure:
1. Victim organization overview
2. Factual summary of the incident
3. Timeline of discovery and disclosure
4. Assessment of attacker motive
5. Analysis of damages and impact
6. Attribution (when available)
7. Professional analysis and opinion

Sources are cited whenever possible, including:
- Company disclosures
- Government advisories (e.g., CISA)
- Security research firms
- Reputable news outlets

---

## Purpose

This repository serves as:
- A cybersecurity learning portfolio
- Practice in incident analysis and risk assessment
- Documentation of real-world security failures and lessons learned

The goal is to demonstrate analytical thinking, clear communication, and applied cybersecurity knowledge.
